### What we build

A B2B event pipeline platform for marketing and revenue teams running 5+ flagship trade shows and conferences a year. Pre-event sourcing, on-floor capture, post-event attribution all flow through one system, with sourced and influenced pipeline writing back into Salesforce or HubSpot within 48 hours of floor close.

The five stages, in order:

1. **Source** the attendee list six weeks out, from public event data, exhibitor directories, speaker lists, and partner portals.
2. **Enrich** through your own Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clay, or Cognism plan. We orchestrate the vendor; you own the data contract.
3. **Sequence** ranked targets into the outbound tool your team already uses, with event-specific angles.
4. **Capture** the floor on the Luminik mobile app: badge scans, voice notes, meeting logs, offline-capable, same-day CRM sync.
5. **Attribute** sourced and influenced pipeline back to Salesforce or HubSpot within 48 hours.

### Who it is for

- **Mid-market B2B SaaS, Series A through C, 50–500 employees, 5+ events per year.** Cybersecurity, fintech, regtech, dev tools, data infra. Teams running RSA, Black Hat, Money20/20, Singapore FinTech Festival, Dreamforce, INBOUND, SaaStr, KubeCon, and the regional events around them.
- VP Marketing or CMO who needs the events budget defended in the QBR with a number that lives in the CRM, not in a vendor dashboard.
- RevOps or Marketing Ops who refuses to route a second data waterfall and pay for the same contact twice.
- Event Marketing Manager who wants the meeting calendar booked six weeks out, not three days before the floor opens.
- AE or SDR walking the floor with the ICP score and pre-event context already in their hand.

### How we are different

- **BYOV** — Bring Your Own Vendor enrichment. You plug in the contracts you already pay for. We do not resell enrichment data and we do not lock you into a closed waterfall.
- **CRM-native.** We run inside your Salesforce or HubSpot. Sourced and influenced pipeline lands as Opportunity custom fields the CFO already trusts, not a sync-target dashboard.
- **End-to-end.** Pre-event, on-floor, post-event, attribution. One platform, one operator surface, one set of integrations.
- **AI drafts. Humans approve.** Our agents draft the sequence, score the ICP, summarise the event. No agent ships an email, posts to LinkedIn, or pushes a CRM sequence without an explicit human click.

### What we are made of

- **Web app**: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + TanStack Query + Tailwind v4
- **Backend**: Kotlin + Quarkus on JDK 21, PostgreSQL, async via SQS
- **Mobile**: Expo + React Native (SDK 54), offline-first, Google ML Kit OCR, Whisper transcription
- **Agent sidecar**: TypeScript on Hono, multi-provider LLM (Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, OpenAI, Google Vertex), evaluator-optimizer + multi-critic + Chain-of-Verification on every customer-facing draft
- **Integrations**: Salesforce, HubSpot, Apollo, Slack, with Outreach next on the calendar
- **Hosted on AWS**, with SOC Type II posture in progress
